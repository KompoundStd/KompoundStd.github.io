NOT A BUG: Idle Defense

It's not a bug, it's a feature.

An idle tower defense on a 90s PC. Malware crawls across your desktop and your programs shoot it down. Buy shortcuts, upgrade everything, fit parts into your PC and type commands into the prompt. Then Format C: and come back stronger.

Wishlist on Steam Play the demo

Windows · Steam  |  Free demo in the browser on itch.io

Agree and install

Every shortcut on your desktop is a turret. Click Ping.exe, Defrag.exe or Paint.exe to buy another copy and watch it hop into place and start firing.

Upgrade both sides

Damage, reload, range, crits: every program has its own upgrade tree. And so does the malware. Bigger packs mean more loot.

Feed the bugs

You let the malware in on purpose. Glitch Beetles, Lag Locusts, Pop-up Wasps and dozens more, each one worth something when it pops.

Debug

Open the Command Prompt and type. FREEZE, BURN, DELTREE: hundreds of bugs gone in one line.

Already won

There's no game over. Whatever gets past your programs melts in the glitch. Leave it running, come back to a fortune.
